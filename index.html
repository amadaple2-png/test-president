Mayor's Choice

A city. A desk. A thousand consequences.

How to Play

Step 1 of 4

Step 1 · You are the Mayor

Each week a new problem lands on your desk, and you decide how the city responds.

Your goal Survive all 8 weeks without letting any of your 4 city meters reach 0.

Keep ALL 4 meters above 0 the whole time, or your time in office is over:

Step 2 · Making Decisions

Every week, a city issue arrives as a memo. Read it, then pick one of two options.

Each option lists its pros and cons. Nearly every choice helps one meter and hurts another, so there is no free lunch.

Example: A factory wants to open

Pros

+20 Money

Cons

−20 Environment

Weigh the trade-offs and manage them wisely. You can’t undo a decision.

Step 3 · Your City Meters

↑ Look at the top bar. These 4 meters show how your city is doing. They all start at 50.

    How scoring works

    If you survive, the values left in the 4 meters at the end are added together to make your Final Score (out of 400).

    e.g. 62 + 48 + 55 + 71 = 236

    Higher meters at the end means a higher score.

    Step 4 · Consequences Come Later

    Some decisions have delayed effects. A choice that looks great today can cause trouble a week or two later.

    Two weeks after approving the factory… Smog hangs over the city. −15 Health−5 Happiness
    Remember
    • Survive all 8 weeks.
    • Keep every meter above 0.
    • End with higher meters for a higher score.
